Scheduling is one of those problems that looks small and compounds fast. A
single meeting can take six messages to land on a time.

## Shared availability

Asynchrone Space calendars know who's on which project, so availability is context-aware.
When you pick a slot, you see conflicts across the people you actually work
with — no separate poll required.

## Meeting cards in context

Every meeting posted to a conversation renders as a card with the time,
duration and attendees. It's a calendar invite that lives where the discussion
happens.

## Fewer meetings, not more

The goal isn't to make scheduling faster — it's to make it rarer. When work is
visible in one place, you often don't need the meeting at all.

Try it on a shared project this week and see how many messages it saves.
