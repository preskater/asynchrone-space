Today we're launching AI-native workflows across Asynchrone Space. Assistants now live inside
your workspace and take on the busywork that slows teams down.

## What's new

Every project, thread and file is now something an assistant can reason about.
That means you can ask for a daily digest, have a thread summarized, or get task
updates drafted automatically.

- **Daily summaries** — a short recap of what changed across your projects.
- **Thread summaries** — catch up on a long conversation in seconds.
- **Task drafting** — turn a conversation into actionable tasks.

## Why it matters

The average team context-switches between five tools a day. Each switch costs
focus. By handling the repetitive work, assistants give that focus back.

> We got a full day back every week. It's the small things — the summaries and
> the reminders — that add up.

## Getting started

AI-native workflows are available on the Team plan and above, and roll out
gradually. Open any project and look for the assistant icon to try it out.

We can't wait to see what you build with it.
